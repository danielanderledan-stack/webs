SEO.

Get found by people about to call someone.

We don't chase "tradie" rankings. We chase "plumber Brighton emergency Sunday" — the searches with a wallet behind them.

01

We target intent, not volume.

"Tradie" gets searched thousands of times a day. It's also searched by students writing a report, journalists writing an article, and backpackers looking for work. Ranking for it costs a fortune and books nothing.

We target "electrician Ballarat switchboard upgrade" or "emergency plumber Carnegie Saturday." The person typing that has a problem right now and a phone in their hand. Ranking first for thirty searches like those beats ranking fourth for one big one, every time.

We map your search terms before the project starts — from real job data, real suburb patterns, real time-of-day behaviour. Pages, headings, and metadata are built around those exact terms.

02

The map pack matters more than the website.

When someone searches "plumber near me," the three businesses in the map pack get most of the calls. The website results underneath are almost irrelevant. Getting into that map pack, and staying there, is a separate discipline from website SEO.

We treat your Google Business Profile as a live asset, not a form you fill in once. New photos every week. Questions answered within 24 hours. Service areas updated when your work changes. Posts that look like they're from a business that's actually on the tools, not a marketing agency.

The review automation feeds this. Google weighs review recency and volume heavily for map pack ranking. A business with 80 recent reviews beats one with 12 old ones, all else equal. Review management isn't just for reputation — it's rank management.

03

The boring bits other builders skip.

Schema markup tells Google exactly what your business is, where it operates, and what it does. Without it, Google guesses. We implement LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, and Review schema across every page. It takes half a day and makes every other piece of SEO work harder.

Page speed under two seconds, measured on a 4G mobile connection. Not "fast enough" — under two. Google uses Core Web Vitals as a ranking signal, and every second of load time costs conversions. Images sized and compressed. Fonts preloaded. Nothing loads that isn't needed.

Alt text on every image, hand-written, describing what's actually in the photo. Sitemaps submitted to Search Console. Canonical tags on any pages that could duplicate. Internal links pointing to the pages that matter most. Not glamorous, but this is the plumbing that makes the visible work pay off.

04

One plain email, once a month.

You don't need a dashboard. You need to know: did we get found for the right searches this month, and did any of those searches turn into jobs? That's what the monthly report covers.

We track keyword positions, map pack ranking, Google Business Profile impressions, and form enquiries in one document. We flag what changed and why. If something isn't working — a page isn't ranking, ads are costing more than they're bringing in — we say it before you ask.

Every three months we look at which jobs converted from search, which suburbs are growing, and whether the search landscape has shifted. Terms that worked six months ago sometimes stop working. We stay on top of it so you don't have to.
