Our process.

The work you don't see.

Most of our work happens before anything goes live. Here's what's actually in the bill.

01 Research 02 Brand 03 Plan 04 Build 05 Systems 06 Publish 07 Ongoing
01

We figure out who actually pays you.

Before we write a word of copy or pick a colour, we spend a week looking at your last twelve months of jobs. Which customers came back. Which suburbs sent the jobs that didn't argue about the price. What people search for at 9pm on a Tuesday — because that's a different intent than 9am on a Saturday.

We build a customer profile that's specific enough to be useful. Not "homeowners aged 35–55." More like: established households in bayside suburbs, two incomes, will pay a premium to avoid a second call-out, found you through a neighbour.

Then we audit your three to five real competitors — not national chains, the operators you actually lose work to. What they rank for, where their reviews fall short, what they say about themselves. That gap is where your positioning goes.

What you get

  • Customer profile document
  • Suburb and demand map
  • 30 intent-heavy search terms
  • Competitor audit
02

What you look like before you look like anything.

Most trade websites look the same because most tradies take the first template they're shown. Orange on navy, a photo of someone in a hard hat, a headline about quality workmanship. It blends into the background before anyone's read a word.

We build a visual identity that fits the customer you're actually targeting. Not just a logo — a system. Colours that hold together across a van wrap, a quote template, and a Google ad. Type that reads well on a phone at arm's length. A voice that sounds like the best version of you — confident, specific, not trying too hard.

The quote template matters more than people expect. It's the first document a customer sees with your name on it. It should look like you take the work seriously.

What you get

  • Colour palette
  • Typeface pairing
  • Logo
  • Voice and tone document
  • Quote and invoice templates
03

We design the whole site before we write a line of code.

Every site problem we've ever fixed came from building before planning. Copy written around a layout, instead of a layout built around the copy. We flip it.

The sitemap comes first — what pages exist and why. Then wireframes in greyscale: every section, every call to action, every bit of navigation, all at low fidelity so we can move things around without burning hours. Copy gets written last, against the wireframe, so it fits exactly where it needs to.

Nothing goes to code until the copy is signed off. That's a rule. It costs us more time upfront. It saves everyone from the "can we just change this paragraph" conversation three weeks into a build.

What you get

  • Sitemap
  • Greyscale wireframes
  • Full copy drafted and signed off
  • Content checklist for assets needed
04

The bit you can see.

Hand-coded HTML and CSS. No Wix, Squarespace, or drag-and-drop builders. They're fast to set up and slow the moment you need to change anything real.

We're on a staging URL by day 10. You can see it on any device, share the link with your partner, open it on your phone in the ute. We go through it section by section before it goes anywhere near your live domain.

The source code is yours. If you leave, you take the files. Lighthouse targets 95+ across the board — performance, accessibility, best practices, SEO. A slow site costs jobs, not just points on a report card.

What you get

  • Staged site within 10 days
  • Cross-browser and device tested
  • Lighthouse 95+ across all four categories
  • Source code files in your hands
05

The bit that does the work.

A website with a "Contact us" form and a phone number is a brochure. It makes the customer do the work. Our job is to turn enquiries into bookings without the phone ringing.

Quote forms collect job details, photos, and site address. The quoting system produces a price within the hour. The customer accepts online. The booking drops into your calendar with the price, the scope, and the address. Payment is built in — card, PayID, bank transfer, or a plan for bigger jobs.

Review requests fire after every completed job. Xero picks up the invoice. SMS and email confirmations go to the customer without you touching anything. We wire it all up in week two.

What you get

  • Quote-to-booking flow
  • Online payments
  • SMS and email automation
  • Calendar sync
  • CRM setup
06

We don't push it live and walk away.

Going live is where most web projects quietly break. DNS migration done wrong takes a site down for hours. Old URLs that Google has indexed go nowhere. Analytics aren't set up, so nobody knows what's working.

We plan the migration in advance, run it outside business hours, and monitor DNS propagation until it's stable. Every URL from your old site redirects to its equivalent page. Google Search Console is set up and the new sitemap submitted within 24 hours of going live.

The first week after launch is the most important. We watch rankings, check for errors, confirm forms are submitting correctly. Day seven you get a report. Most tradies don't get this — they get a "it's live" email and never hear from the developer again.

What you get

  • Zero-downtime DNS migration
  • Redirect map for all old URLs
  • Google Analytics and Search Console hookup
  • Day-seven launch report
07

Month two onwards. This is what the fee is actually for.

Building the site is the start. Everything after it is what moves the phone.

We run Google Ads on a weekly cycle — not set and forget. Ad copy gets tested. Search terms wasting money get cut. Budget shifts to what converts. The goal is a cost-per-job that makes the maths obvious.

Monthly: one piece of content based on what your customers actually search for. Quarterly: a site refresh based on which jobs converted, what customers asked, where the gaps are. Every finished job triggers a review request, followed up twice. Any bad rating gets a response and a call before it settles on your profile.

What you get

  • Weekly ad optimisation
  • Monthly content
  • Quarterly site refresh
  • Monthly report
  • A named contact who works your account
